Validating RDF with RDF

One of the questions that kept me busy during my journey on understanding RDF, was the relationship between what I now call the knowledge representation view on RDF, and the data management view on RDF. In this post, I'll lay out my understanding between the two views, and some insights based on this understanding, in particular in context of my specialty, validation and SHACL.

Let's start with examining what an RDF triple is exactly. Consider the following RDF triple: 

:maxime :likes :chilly_gonzales

According to RDF 1.1 Semantics, we can associate an interpretation to this small RDF graph which interprets the tuple (:maxime, :chilly\_gonzales) as part of the :likes relation.

RDF 1.1 interpretations are a complex structure, it allows for special interpretation of some triples, like for example: 

:chilly_gonzales rdf:type :musician

which should be interpreted as :chilly\_gonzales being part of the extension of the class :musician. There are other such special interpretations of RDF triples, but this gives you the basic idea.

In this sense, every RDF triple can be seen as a logical statement, which we can associate an interpretation with. This is then extended to RDFS and OWL.

However, SHACL and SPARQL view things differently.

Concluding Remarks

I should stress that this post expresses my understanding of the situation, and I'll happily address any misconceptions that I might have unwittingly expressed here.
